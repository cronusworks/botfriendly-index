How the index works

For people: browse, filter, and read listings with the numbers up front — asking price, revenue, cash flow and how that cash flow figure was calculated, plus whether tax returns are available and whether the broker is NDA-ready.

For search: the same facts are published as a standardized record for every listing — the format modern search and screening tools surface first. Every record carries published, verified-as-of, and review-by dates, and contact is relay-only: inquiries come through the index, and broker contact details are never published.

For buyers: registered buyers publish standardized buy-box profiles. Buyer #1 is live — see the Buyers page.
