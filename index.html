How the two doors work

For people: browse, filter, and read listings with the numbers up front — asking price, revenue, cash flow and how that cash flow figure was calculated, plus whether tax returns are available and whether the broker is NDA-ready.

For agents: the same facts live at listings.json, in llms.txt / llms-full.txt, and as schema.org JSON-LD on every page. Crawlers are explicitly welcomed in robots.txt. Every record carries published, verified-as-of, and review-by dates, and contact is relay-only — agents get facts, brokers keep their privacy.

For buyers: registered buyers publish agent-readable buy-box profiles. Buyer #1 is live — see the Buyers page.
