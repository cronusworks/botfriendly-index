# Bot-Friendly Business Index

Businesses for sale, published for people AND their AI agents — one site, two doors, same truth.

- Human door: browsable listings with the numbers up front (price, revenue, cash flow **and its basis**, tax-return and NDA flags, freshness dates, badge tiers).
- Agent door: [`llms.txt`](https://cronusworks.github.io/botfriendly-index/llms.txt), [`llms-full.txt`](https://cronusworks.github.io/botfriendly-index/llms-full.txt), [`listings.json`](https://cronusworks.github.io/botfriendly-index/listings.json) (one structured record per listing), per-listing `listing.json`, [`agents.json`](https://cronusworks.github.io/botfriendly-index/agents.json), schema.org JSON-LD on every page, and a `robots.txt` that explicitly welcomes AI crawlers.

Every listing follows the published [Agent-Readiness Standard](https://cronusworks.github.io/botfriendly-index/standard/). Contact is relay-only — no raw broker contact details in any page or feed.

Status: validation launch (Nov 13, 2026). The three launch listings are clearly marked SAMPLE demonstrations; real broker submissions are open and free during validation via the [submission form](https://cronusworks.github.io/botfriendly-index/submit/) (FormSubmit relay to the index team's inbox).
