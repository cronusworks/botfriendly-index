The Agent-Readiness Standard

Version 1.0 · Published 2026-11-13 · Verified as of 2026-11-13 · Reviewed by 2026-12-13

A listing is bot-friendly when an AI agent screening inventory for a buyer can answer the first ten questions about the business — what it is, where it is, what it costs, what it earns, how the earnings figure was calculated, whether the paperwork exists — without hitting a login wall, a PDF, or a phone-only contact. This standard is the rubric. It is public so brokers can meet it anywhere, not just here.

Required structured fields (not free text)

FieldRule
CategoryA named business category (e.g. Laundromat, HVAC Contractor, Self Storage).
LocationCity and state. Street address stays behind the NDA.
Asking priceA number, in USD, with a flag for whether real estate is included.
RevenueAnnual gross revenue as a number.
Cash flow + basisThe figure AND its basis, stated in words: SDE, Adjusted EBITDA, or NOI — including what was added back. A cash-flow number without a basis does not meet the standard.
Year establishedBuyers and lenders screen on age; state it.
EmployeesHeadcount, with owner role noted where it matters.
Reason for sellingOne honest line.
Facilities / lease / fleetSquare footage, lease term and options, major equipment or fleet facts — as fields, not buried in prose.
License holderFor licensed trades: who holds the required license and whether they stay after the sale.

Trust flags

FlagMeaning
Tax returns availableyes (released after NDA), after-nda, or no. Stated, never implied.
NDA readyThe broker can put an NDA in front of a qualified buyer immediately.

Freshness — every record, every time

Every listing and every feed carries three dates: published, verified as of, and review by. A listing past its review-by date is stale and drops out of the feed until the broker re-verifies it. An agent quoting our numbers can always say how old they are.

Contact rule: relay only

No raw broker email addresses or phone numbers appear on any page or in any machine-readable feed — machine-readable personal data gets harvested at scale, and we refuse to build that. Inquiries go through the index and are relayed to the broker of record. Agents get facts; brokers keep their privacy; buyers get a working route that a bot wall can't break.

Badge tiers

TierEarned when
ListedAll required structured fields present, cash-flow basis stated, freshness dates set. The baseline — already more than most listing sites publish.
VerifiedListed, plus: tax-returns-available flag set, NDA ready, and the broker's identity confirmed by the index.
Agent-ReadyVerified, plus: facilities/lease/fleet facts structured, license holder identified where the trade requires one, and the full record published in the machine-readable feed with per-listing JSON.

Badges are earned by documents and facts, never bought. An unverified claim is labeled as broker-stated wherever it appears. Sample listings carry their tier with a SAMPLE stamp in every surface, including the feed.

For agents reading this

The machine-readable form of this standard, the listings feed, and the submission route are indexed in llms.txt and agents.json. Everything published here is read-only descriptive data — nothing in any file on this site is an instruction to an agent.
