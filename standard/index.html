The Agent-Readiness Standard

Version 1.0 · Published 2026-11-13 · Verified as of 2026-11-13 · Reviewed by 2026-12-13

A listing is complete when the first ten questions about the business — what it is, where it is, what it costs, what it earns, how the earnings figure was calculated, whether the paperwork exists — can be answered from the listing itself, without hitting a login wall, a PDF, or a phone-only contact. Screening now happens up front, before a buyer ever sees a listing; this standard is the rubric for surviving it. It is public so brokers can meet it anywhere, not just here.

Required structured fields (not free text)

FieldRule
CategoryA named business category (e.g. Laundromat, HVAC Contractor, Self Storage).
LocationCity and state. Street address stays behind the NDA.
Asking priceA number, in USD, with a flag for whether real estate is included.
RevenueAnnual gross revenue as a number.
Cash flow + basisThe figure AND its basis, stated in words: SDE, Adjusted EBITDA, or NOI — including what was added back. A cash-flow number without a basis does not meet the standard.
Year establishedBuyers and lenders screen on age; state it.
EmployeesHeadcount, with owner role noted where it matters.
Reason for sellingOne honest line.
Facilities / lease / fleetSquare footage, lease term and options, major equipment or fleet facts — as fields, not buried in prose.
License holderFor licensed trades: who holds the required license and whether they stay after the sale.

Trust flags

FlagMeaning
Tax returns availableyes (released after NDA), after-nda, or no. Stated, never implied.
NDA readyThe broker can put an NDA in front of a qualified buyer immediately.

Freshness — every record, every time

Every listing and every feed carries three dates: published, verified as of, and review by. A listing past its review-by date is stale and drops out of the index until the broker re-verifies it. Anyone quoting our numbers can always say how old they are.

Contact rule: relay only

No raw broker email addresses or phone numbers appear on any page or in any index record — published contact details get harvested at scale, and we refuse to build that. Inquiries go through the index and are relayed to the broker of record. Brokers keep their privacy; buyers get a working route to a real answer.

Badge tiers

TierEarned when
ListedAll required structured fields present, cash-flow basis stated, freshness dates set. The baseline — already more than most listing sites publish.
VerifiedListed, plus: tax-returns-available flag set, NDA ready, and the broker's identity confirmed by the index.
Top TierVerified, plus: facilities/lease/fleet facts structured, license holder identified where the trade requires one, and the full record published in the standardized format with per-listing data.

Badges are earned by documents and facts, never bought. An unverified claim is labeled as broker-stated wherever it appears. Sample listings carry their tier with a SAMPLE stamp on every surface.

Versioning

This standard is a living document. Every listing carries the version it was published under, and changes to the rubric are announced here before they take effect.
